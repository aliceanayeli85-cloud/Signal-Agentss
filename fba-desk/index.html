    How to find product ideas (free)
    1. Browse Amazon's Best Sellers and Movers & Shakers lists in categories you understand.
    2. Note items whose price looks high enough to leave room for fees, then add them here as ideas.
    3. Read the top listings: many sellers with thousands of reviews means strong competition. Repeated complaints in reviews can point to a better version you could offer.
    4. Look up supplier prices for similar items and ask for written quotes.
    5. Until you know the rules, avoid categories that need Amazon's approval, hazardous items, and oversized items. Amazon's seller help pages explain which ones these are.
    6. Fill in each idea with the "How to find this" tips, save it, and let your rules sort the list.

    General tips only. This app does not recommend specific products.
