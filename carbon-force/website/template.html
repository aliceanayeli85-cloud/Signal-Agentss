FAQ

Before you book

Do you come to me?

No. Carbon Force is drop-off only. The setup is in a garage in Melrose Park, so you bring the car in between 9 and 11am, Monday to Saturday.

How long does it take?

You get it back the same day. A full detail can run into the evening. You'll get a text as soon as it's done.

Can you get this stain out?

Text a photo and you'll get an honest answer. Fresh spills usually come out well. Old, set-in stains sometimes don't, and you'll hear that before you book.

Is dog hair extra?

Sometimes. Send a photo before you book and you'll get an exact price, so there are no surprises at pickup.

What services don't you offer?

Right now: no paint correction, polishing, ceramic coatings, carpet extraction or mobile service. If your car needs one of those, you'll be told up front.

How do I pay?

Apple Cash, Zelle or cash when you pick up your car.
