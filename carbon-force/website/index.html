Questions

Good to know

Do you come to me?

No, I'm drop-off only. My setup is in my garage in Melrose Park, so you bring the car to me between 9 and 11am, Monday to Saturday.

How long does it take?

You get it back the same day. A full detail can take until the evening. I text you as soon as it's done.

Can you get this stain out?

Text me a photo and I'll tell you honestly. Fresh spills usually come out well. Old, set-in stains sometimes don't, and I'd rather tell you that up front.

My car has a lot of dog hair. Is that extra?

Sometimes. Send a photo before you book and I'll quote you an exact price, so there are no surprises when you pick it up.

What don't you do?

Right now I don't do paint correction, polishing, ceramic coatings, carpet extraction or mobile service. If your car needs one of those, I'll tell you.

How do I pay?

Apple Cash, Zelle or cash when you pick up your car.
