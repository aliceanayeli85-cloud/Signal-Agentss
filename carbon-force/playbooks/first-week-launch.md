# First-week launch plan

**Goal:** get the first paying customers from people near you who can drop off a car.
**Time:** about 30-60 minutes a day. **Money:** $0.

Nothing below is done yet. Tick each item off as **you** do it.

## Day 0: Setup (before launch)
- [ ] Start the app (`python run.py`) and fill in **all of Settings**. Set **launch date** = tomorrow.
- [ ] Put your logo in `brand/logo.png`, and your flyer files in `brand/flyers/`.
- [ ] Call Village Hall (708-343-4000) and ask about home-business rules and signs. Write down the answer in Prospecting.
- [ ] Add your **real** open drop-off slots for the next 7 days (Booking page).
- [ ] Approve posts Day 1 (open for business), Day 2 (price menu) and Day 3 (drop-off FAQ).

## Day 1: Exist online
- [ ] Create the **Facebook Page** (Prospecting row has the checklist). Logo, flyer, About = prices + "drop-off only, Melrose Park".
- [ ] Publish **Day 1 post** on the Page, then mark it published.
- [ ] Share the Page from your personal profile with one line: "Started a detailing setup in my garage. Prices are on the page." (No pressure to tag anyone.)
- [ ] Text 5-10 people you actually know (friends, family, coworkers): what you do, the prices, and "if you know anyone." These are people you know, so it's not spam.
- [ ] Every reply → add as a lead (source: `referral` or `other`).

## Day 2: Price clarity + neighbors
- [ ] Publish **Day 2 price menu** and **pin it** to the top of the Page.
- [ ] Find 2-3 **real** local Facebook groups (Melrose Park and the nearest towns). Add each as its own Prospecting row. Read the rules and set the rule type. Request to join.
- [ ] Reply to every lead the same day.

## Day 3: Instagram + drop-off FAQ
- [ ] Set up Instagram (`playbooks/instagram-setup.md`). Post Day 1 and Day 2 there too.
- [ ] Publish **Day 3 "Do you come to me?"** on Facebook.
- [ ] In any group you've been accepted into: if business posts are allowed, post the **group version** of Day 1. If only recommendations are allowed, search the group for "detail" / "car wash" and answer real requests with the recommendation template.

## Day 4: Partners and flyers
- [ ] Pick ONE nearby auto repair or tire shop. Visit in person with flyers, using the partner intro template as your script. Record it.
- [ ] Ask at 2 places with public bulletin boards (library, laundromat) if you can pin a flyer. Record each one.
- [ ] Follow up on any quotes from Days 1-3.

## Day 5: Fill real open slots
- [ ] If you still have open slots this week, post **"Open drop-off times this week"** with those exact times (Page + Story + groups that allow it on that day).
- [ ] Send reminders for tomorrow's appointments.

## Day 6: First jobs = first content
- [ ] On every job: before/after photos from the same angle, plus 1-2 short close-up clips. Hands and car only, plates blurred.
- [ ] Ask the customer: "Mind if I post before/after pics? No plate, no name."
- [ ] Record the job and payment in the app.

## Day 7: Review
- [ ] Day after each job: send the review request (once).
- [ ] Open **Weekly**. Note which channel produced inquiries.
- [ ] Approve next week's 3 posts. Back up.
- [ ] Cash still tight? Turn on **Cash mode** for week 2.

## What "working" looks like after week 1
You're measuring **inquiries → quotes → bookings → paid jobs**, by channel. Week 1 might bring 0 jobs
or several, and both are possible for a brand-new business. The point of week 1 is that every channel
you tried is recorded, so week 2 puts more time into whatever actually got people to message you.
