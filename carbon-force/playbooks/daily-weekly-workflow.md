# Daily and weekly workflow

The **Today** page shows all of this automatically. This file is the same routine on paper.

## Every day (10-20 min, twice a day if you can: morning + evening)
1. **Replies first.** New leads get a reply the same day. Fast replies win small local jobs more than anything else.
2. **Follow-ups due.** Max 2 follow-ups per person, then mark them Lost (reason: no response).
3. **Today's plan:** one content action + one customer action + one follow-up action. The optional task is optional.
4. **Log it.** New inquiry = add lead. Posted something = mark published. Spent 20 min in groups = log effort.

## The 7-day routine
| Day | Content | Get customers | Follow up | Optional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Show up | 1 Page post | Find 2 groups, read rules, ask to join | Reply to all new leads | 5 min close-up video on a job |
| 2 Be findable | 1 Story | 1 Google/search/Page-setup step | First nudges due | Pin price post |
| 3 Neighbors | 1 Page post (+ group version if allowed) | Answer real "need a detailer" requests in groups | Follow-ups due | Flyers on 1 public board |
| 4 Partners | Reel if you have footage | 1 local business, in person or via public contact | Quote follow-ups | Add next week's open slots |
| 5 Fill the week | "Open times" post (real slots only) | Repeat reminders to past customers | Tomorrow's reminders | 1 referral ask |
| 6 Work day | Shoot before/after + clips | Ask customers' OK to post photos | Review requests | Approve next week's posts |
| 7 Review | No posting needed | Read Weekly report and drop what didn't work | Close dead leads, back up | Log forgotten effort |

Posting load: about 3-4 posts a week. That's enough to stay visible without posting constantly.

## CASH MODE (when you need paying jobs this week)
1. Reply to every New Lead right now.
2. Send every follow-up due today (quote follow-ups first).
3. Add your real open slots for the next 7 days.
4. Repeat-offer texts to past customers only.
5. Referral asks to 2-3 happy past customers.
6. "Open drop-off times" post with your real slots: Page, plus groups whose rules allow it today.
7. Answer real recommendation requests in your groups.
8. Skip everything that won't lead to a message today (new Reels, logo tweaks, more research).

## Weekly review (Day 7, 10 min)
1. Weekly page → read "What to change next week."
2. Which channel brought inquiries that turned into **paid** jobs?
3. Which channel took time and brought nothing? Cut it in half.
   Exception: Google listings take weeks to start working.
4. Approve 2-3 posts for next week. Add next week's real open slots.
5. Back up. Copy `data/backups/` somewhere safe.

## Lead stages
New Lead (no reply yet) → Replied → Quote Sent → *(Follow-Up Due when the date arrives)* → Booked → Completed.
Lost is the exit for anyone who stops replying, needs mobile service, or wants a service you don't offer.
