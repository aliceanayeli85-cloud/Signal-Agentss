# Carbon Force Detailing: brand voice

**One line:** a local person who's good at cleaning cars, tells you the price straight, and doesn't oversell.

## Sounds like
- **Plain and specific.** "Crumbs in the seat creases" beats "restore your interior to showroom condition."
- **Price up front.** Never "DM for price." The prices are $80 / $60 / $140, so say them.
- **Honest about limits.** "Send me a photo and I'll tell you what to expect" instead of promising a stain comes out.
- **Dry and a little funny, sometimes.** About one post in four. Joke about the car, never about the customer.
- **Short, then a longer sentence, then short again.** Like talking.
- **"I", not "we."** It's a one-person shop, and that's a selling point.

## Doesn't sound like
- "Elevate your ride!" / "Your car deserves the best!" / "Experience the difference!"
- Rows of emojis. One is fine now and then; zero is fine always.
- 15 hashtags. Use 3-5 local ones on Instagram and none on Facebook.
- Fake urgency: "Only 2 spots left!", "Booking up fast!" Only say you have openings when you have them,
  and list the real times.
- Made-up stories: "A customer told me..." only if one actually did.

## Hard rules (the app enforces most of these)
| Never say | Because |
| --- | --- |
| mobile / we come to you | Drop-off only |
| ceramic, polishing, paint correction, extraction | Not offered (yet) |
| insured, certified, #1, best in... | Not confirmed |
| fully booked, limited spots, X spots left | Only with real, current numbers |
| reviews or testimonials you didn't receive | Fake reviews are dishonest and break platform rules |
| before/after photos that aren't yours | Use your own work only |

## Quick test before posting
1. Would I say this out loud to a neighbor?
2. Is every fact in it true today?
3. Does it say what to do next (text, DM, ask for times)?
4. Did I already post the same opening line this month?

## Words to reuse
drop-off · garage · Melrose Park · inside and out · honest answer · send me a photo · open drop-off times · clean car, simple
