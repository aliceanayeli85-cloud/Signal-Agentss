# Instagram setup (not created yet)

Free. About 20 minutes. Do this on Day 3 of the launch, after the Facebook Page exists.

## 1. Create it
- Handle ideas (check what's available): `carbonforcedetailing`, `carbonforce.detailing`, `carbonforcedetail`
- Profile photo: your existing logo.
- Switch to a **Professional account → Business** (free). Category: Car Wash or Automotive Service.
- Contact: your business phone and/or email. Leave the address off, or show only "Melrose Park, IL".
- Don't buy followers. Don't boost posts (that costs money).

## 2. Bio (150 characters max; pick one)
```
Drop-off car detailing in Melrose Park, IL
Interior $80 / Exterior $60 / Both $140
Not mobile. DM or text to book
```
```
One-person detailing shop. Melrose Park garage, drop-off only.
$80 in / $60 out / $140 both. DM to book
```

## 3. Story highlights (make these from Stories)
- **Prices** (Day 2 graphic)
- **Booking** (Day 10's four steps)
- **Before/After** (your real ones only, added as you go)
- **FAQ** ("Do you come to me?", "How long?")

## 4. Before you tell anyone it exists
Post 3 things first so the profile doesn't look empty: Day 1 intro, Day 2 prices, and one real
photo or Reel of your work (or the flyer). Then add the Instagram link to the Facebook Page.

## 5. Each week
- 1-2 feed posts or Reels from the library, plus a few Stories. That's enough.
- Reply to DMs the same day, and add every DM inquiry as a lead with source `instagram`.
- Hashtags: 3-5 local ones (`#melrosepark #westsuburbs #cardetailing #chicagosuburbs`). Never 30.
