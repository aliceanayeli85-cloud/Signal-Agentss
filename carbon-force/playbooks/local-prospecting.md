# Local prospecting: Melrose Park and nearby

**Priority:** people who can easily drive a car to your garage. Closest first: Melrose Park, Stone Park,
Maywood, Bellwood, Franklin Park, Elmwood Park, River Grove, Northlake, Berkeley, Broadview, Westchester, River Forest.

**Status of everything below: not created, not joined, not contacted.** The Prospecting page in the
app tracks each item from "to research" through "contacted" to the outcome. Only update a status after
you've actually done the thing.

## Ground rules
- Follow each platform's and each group's rules. If the rules are unclear, ask an admin or just don't post.
- Public business contacts only (the phone on their sign or website, or walking in). No personal numbers.
- No scraping member lists, no buying leads, no mass DMs, no copy-paste blasts across 10 groups in a day.
- Record: source, date, opportunity, next action, outcome. The app has a field for each.

## 1. Facebook groups and community pages
**What I found (Oct 2026):** the Village of Melrose Park has promoted an official Facebook group and Instagram
page (melrosepark.org news post, 2022). I did **not** verify any resident-run groups. You'll need to search for those.

Search: `Melrose Park IL`, `Melrose Park neighbors`, `Melrose Park moms`, plus each nearby town name.

For each real group, add a row and set its **rule type**:
| Rule type | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| promo_allowed | Post the post's **group version**, at most once a week, with a real photo. |
| promo_day_only | Only post in the promo thread or on the promo day. |
| recommendations_only | Don't post ads. Search "detail", "car wash", "clean my car" and answer real requests once, using the recommendation reply template. |
| no_business | Don't post. Be a normal member. |
| unknown | Read the rules first. |

## 2. Google Business Profile: check eligibility first
From Google's own guidelines (support.google.com/business/answer/3038177, checked Oct 2026):
- A profile that **shows an address** needs **permanent signage** with the business name at that location.
- Service-area profiles (hidden address) are for businesses that **go to customers**. You don't, so don't
  use that option just to hide your address. It would misrepresent the business.
- The name must be exactly "Carbon Force Detailing" (no keywords like "Car Detailing Melrose Park").

**Your options:**
1. Find out from the Village whether a permanent sign is allowed at your home. If yes, and you're OK with
   your address being public, create the profile showing the address.
2. Skip Google for now and lean on Facebook, Instagram, groups and referrals. You can revisit it later.

Privacy note: a public profile puts your home address on Google Maps. Decide that on purpose.

## 3. Organic local search (free)
- Use the same name, phone and wording everywhere (Facebook, Instagram, Nextdoor, flyers).
- Facebook Page "About": "Drop-off car detailing in Melrose Park, IL. Interior $80, exterior $60, both $140."
- Bing Places and Apple Business Connect are free, with the same address/sign question as Google.
- Yelp: free page is OK. **Never ask for Yelp reviews.** Yelp's rules prohibit asking for them.

## 4. Partnerships and referrals
| Who | Why they'd care | First step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent auto repair / tire shops | Customers ask about cleaning. You're not competition. | Walk in with flyers. Partner intro template. |
| Small used-car lots | Clean cars photograph better for listings | Ask the manager. Be honest about your daily max. |
| Places with public bulletin boards (library, laundromats, grocery) | Free local visibility | Ask staff before pinning anything |
| Melrose Park Chamber (mpcccd.org, 708-343-9500) | Local business network | Membership has dues. Decide before joining. |
| Village-wide garage sales (3rd weekend, May-Sept) | Neighbors out walking | Revisit in spring |

Only make a referral reward promise ("$10 off for referrals") if you've actually decided to offer it.
If you do, add it to Settings / templates yourself.

## 5. Customers you already have
The cheapest source. Built into every lead:
- Day after the job → review request (once, never in exchange for anything)
- A few days later → referral ask
- About 5 weeks later → repeat-booking reminder, then every 60 days
