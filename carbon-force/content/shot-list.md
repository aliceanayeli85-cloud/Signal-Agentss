# Shot list: faceless photos and video

Your hands and the car only. No faces, no license plates (blur them), no house numbers,
no customer names or mail on the seats. Ask the customer before posting their car.

## Every job (5 minutes total)
| # | Shot | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Before: driver's-door interior wide | Phone at chest height, door fully open |
| 2 | Before: worst spot (floor mat, cup holder, back seat) | Close, good light |
| 3 | Before: exterior three-quarter front | Same spot you'll use for the after |
| 4-6 | **After**: the exact same 3 shots | Same angle, same light, no filters |
| 7 | One 10-second close-up clip | See the "satisfying" list below |

**Same angle, same light** is what makes a before/after believable. Mark where you stood with tape on the garage floor.

## Satisfying close-ups (5-15 seconds each, phone propped up)
- Vacuum crevice tool between the seat and the console
- Sliding the seat forward to show what's under it, then the clean floor
- Brush in a cup holder, then a towel wipe
- Brush through wheel spokes, then rinse
- Floor mat before and after, flat on the garage floor
- Wiping the inside of a windshield (shoot through the glass)
- Water sheeting off a panel during the rinse
- Timelapse of a full interior through the open passenger door

## Phone settings
- Clean the lens. Seriously.
- Shoot vertical (9:16) for Reels and Stories, square or vertical for feed photos.
- Lock focus and exposure (tap and hold on iPhone) so the light doesn't jump.
- 1x zoom, not digital zoom. Move closer instead.
- Garage lights on plus daylight from the open door, if you can.

## Making a Reel with no voice
1. Pick 3-5 clips (2-5 seconds each).
2. Add the text from the Reel script in the post library (big, short, high contrast).
3. Audio: a trending instrumental from Instagram's audio library, or just the real sounds.
4. First 2 seconds = the dirtiest frame. Last 2 seconds = the cleanest.

## Never
- Stock photos or anyone else's before/afters
- Photos of a job you didn't do
- Editing that changes how clean something looks (filters that hide dirt)
